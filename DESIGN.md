# Design System: PixelCI

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Review Desk"**

A tidy, well-lit workstation: white cards on a cool grey desk, one indigo accent for the action, status in small coloured tags; screenshots are the papers being reviewed. Everything on the desk is furniture for looking at two images side by side and deciding. The chrome is a 46px bar, a page title, and a stack of cards; the screenshots get whatever width is left.

The shipped UI is compact, quiet, and utilitarian. Compact: body text is 14px, controls are 32px tall, tags are 19px, and one list row carries branch, commit, author, date, and status. Quiet: surfaces are near-neutral greys with hairline borders, resting shadows are barely visible, and hover only shifts a background by a step. Utilitarian: there is no illustration and no imagery other than the user's own screenshots; the single decorative moment is a faint grid behind the sign-in card.

The system ships in light and dark from one set of token names. Every colour is a CSS custom property declared for light on the root and redeclared for dark, so components never branch on theme. The theme follows the operating system unless the user picks Light or Dark from the theme menu. The tokens and primitives were copied from an internal "CI Dashboard" design system, so a few names and unused primitives come from that origin rather than from this product. The brand appears as a small grid glyph in an inverted rounded square beside the text wordmark "PixelCI"; there is no separate logo asset.

**Key Characteristics:**

- White cards (12px radius, hairline border, faint shadow) on a cool grey page.
- One indigo accent, used for the committing action, the selected or on state, and focus.
- Status is always a small tag: soft fill, matching border, darker text of the same tone, with a word in it.
- Compact scale: 14px body, 13px controls, 12px meta, 11px tags; hierarchy comes from weight more than size.
- A translucent, blurred top bar (46px) with a sticky page header directly beneath it.
- Light and dark themes share one token vocabulary; dark status fills are translucent tints of the tone.
- Screenshots sit on a slightly darker mat. Fit never scales one above its natural size; 200% is the one deliberate magnification and stays pixel-sharp.
- The review screen keeps the build decision in a sticky bar at the bottom of the viewport and can be worked from the keyboard.

## Colors

A cool, near-neutral grey scale carries almost everything; one indigo family marks action and focus, and six status tones appear only as tag and notice triplets. Each token has a dark twin with the same name plus `-dark` in the frontmatter (in code the same custom property is redeclared under the dark class). Values below are light / dark.

### Primary

- **Action Indigo** (`accent`: #4f46e5 / #7c74ff): fill of the primary button, the checked checkbox and the on state of the admin toggle, the loading spinner, the toast progress bar, the check beside the selected theme, and the skip link.
- **Action Indigo Hover** (`accent-hover`: #4338ca / #918aff): primary button hover. Darker in light, lighter in dark.
- **On Indigo** (`accent-text`: #ffffff / #0c0d10): text on the accent fill. White in light; the page black in dark, because the dark accent is light enough to need dark text.
- **Focus Halo** (`accent-soft`: rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.1) / rgba(124, 116, 255, 0.14)): the 3px ring around a focused input, select, or search field, and the faint glow at the top of the sign-in page.
- **Focus Ring Indigo** (`focus`: #6366f1 / #8b85ff): the 2px keyboard focus outline on every focusable element, the border of a focused field, and the ring around the active card on the review screen.
- **Link Indigo** (`link`: #3d37c9 / #a7a2ff): text links, and the hover colour of commit links.

### Named Rules

**The One Indigo Rule.** Indigo means "this is the action, this is selected, or this has focus". It fills one committing button per surface (the page header, a dialog's action row, or the review screen's decision bar), marks checked and on states, and draws focus. It never carries status.

**The Tag Triplet Rule.** A status tone is always applied as its three tokens together: soft fill, matching border, tone-coloured text. Tags are never solid fills, and every tag contains a word, so colour is never the only signal.

**The Borrowed Fill Rule.** The Approve and Reject buttons are the only controls that wear a status triplet (success and danger). Everywhere else, buttons are neutral or indigo and status stays in tags.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inter (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Inter, the same stack. There is one typeface for everything.
**Label/Mono Font:** ui-monospace (with 'SF Mono', 'JetBrains Mono', Menlo, Consolas, monospace). Not loaded; it resolves to the platform's monospace.

**Character:** One sans-serif at small sizes, set tight and even. The body turns on Inter's single-storey a (`cv11`), open digits (`ss01`), and tabular numerals (`tnum`), so hashes, dates, and counts line up in lists. Inter is self-hosted through `@fontsource/inter` as four static Latin weights (400, 500, 600, 700). Icons are Font Awesome Free, loaded as an icon font.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (650, 20px, 1.45, -0.015em): the title on the sign-in and first-run cards only.
- **Headline** (650, 19px, 1.45, -0.01em): the page title in the sticky header ("Apps", or the app name on the builds and review pages). One per page. On the review page it truncates with an ellipsis past 320px.
- **Title** (600, 17px, 1.45): the heading of a form dialog and of the submit confirm.
- **Card title** (600, 14px, 1.45): the name in a list row or screenshot card (app, screen, user, integration). Same size as body; weight does the work. The wordmark in the top bar is this size at weight 650.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.45): default text, including the next-step line of an empty state.
- **Body small** (400, 13px, 1.45): subtitles, notices, menu items, toast text, checkbox labels, comment previews. The progress text in the decision bar is this size at weight 500 in Ink.
- **Control** (500, 13px, line-height 1): button labels, segmented options, nav links, and form field labels (which keep the inherited 1.45 line height). Small buttons drop to 12px; large buttons rise to 14px.
- **Caption** (400, 12px, 1.45): metadata. Dates, build counts, commit subjects in lists, the branch and commit line of the review header, the shortcut legend, hints, tooltips.
- **Tag** (500, 11px, line-height 1): tag text.
- **Label caps** (600, 11px, 0.045em, uppercase, Meta Grey): the "Reference build" and "New build" column labels over a comparison, and the group label at the top of a dropdown menu (0.04em there). Nowhere else. A qualifier after a column label ("approved baseline", or the note that a new screen has no diff) drops back to regular weight and sentence case at the same size.
- **Mono** (400, 12px in code blocks and the private key field; 0.92em for inline code and the hash in the review header; 11px in key caps): commit hashes, screen number tags, CI snippets, callback URLs, and the keys in the shortcut legend.

### Named Rules

**The Weight Before Size Rule.** The whole ramp sits between 11px and 20px. Step up a level by weight (400, 500, 600, 650) before reaching for a larger size; a row title and its body text are both 14px.

**The Machine Text Rule.** Text a machine produced and a person may copy (hashes, IDs, snippets, URLs, keys) is monospace. Everything a person wrote is Inter.

## Layout

A single column under a full-width top bar. The top bar is sticky, 46px tall (`--header-h`), with 16px side padding. Each page is a centred container (max 1120px, padding 28px 20px 48px) holding a page header and then cards. The page header (title left, actions right, wrapping when tight) is sticky directly under the top bar, with 12px vertical padding, a 20px gap below, and the page background behind it. Lists live inside one card as rows separated by hairlines, not as separate cards. Dialogs are a 520px column with 24px padding and a 16px gap between sections (440px for the submit confirm); form fields stack with a 12px gap and 6px between a label and its control.

The review screen breaks the container: it always runs full width so the two screenshots get every pixel. Its sticky header has three groups on one row with 12px gaps. On the left, the back button and the app name. In the middle, a context line that takes the spare width: branch, short hash with commit subject, and the build's status tag. On the right, the tools: the filter segmented control, the Diff checkbox, and the zoom segmented control. The context line gives way first when space runs out: the commit subject truncates, then disappears once the line is narrower than 420px, then the branch truncates (never below 56px). At 1120px and below the context line moves to its own full-width row under the title and tools.

Screenshot cards stack with a 16px gap. Inside a card the comparison is two equal columns with a 6px gap, each a pane that scrolls on its own. The two panes are locked: scrolling or panning either one moves the other to the same offset on both axes. A pane is never taller than the space between the sticky header and the decision bar (viewport height minus both, minus 196px for the card's own chrome, and never under 200px), so a card brought to the top shows its name, both screenshots, and its review bar at once. Both sticky heights are measured live, because the header and the bar change height as they wrap. Zoom has three modes, remembered per app: Fit scales the screenshot down to the column and never above its natural size; 100% shows one image pixel per CSS pixel; 200% doubles that with pixelated rendering. In the two pixel modes the pane scrolls instead of the image shrinking. An unchanged screen, when expanded, shows one pane with the new screenshot, centred and at most 1280px wide.

The decision bar is sticky at the bottom of the review screen (12px vertical padding, 16px above it, gaps of 8px by 20px): progress text on the left, the shortcut legend filling the middle, the one primary button pushed to the right. It appears only while the build is awaiting review and has screens to review. The review header and the decision bar each reach 4px past the content edge so the active card's ring slides under them instead of showing beside them. A card that is scrolled to lands 12px clear of the sticky header and the decision bar.

There are no spacing custom properties. The recurring literal values are 6px (label to control, between the two panes), 8px (between buttons), 12px (form rows, header items), 14px and 18px (vertical and horizontal padding inside cards and rows), 16px (between cards, admin rows), 20px and 28px (page gutters). Controls share one height (32px) so any mix of button, input, select, and segmented control aligns on a row.

Responsive behaviour is desktop-first with four max-width breakpoints:

- **1120px:** the review header's context line moves to its own row.
- **900px:** the five-column build row (branch, commit, author, date, status) becomes two columns with status moved to the top right and the commit line spanning full width; the decision bar wraps.
- **640px:** page padding drops to 16px 12px 32px, the wordmark hides and leaves the mark, dialog padding drops to 18px, the auth card to 24px 20px, the screenshot card to 14px 12px, and the review bar stacks the comment above the buttons. On the review screen the header's title and tools wrap with a 10px gap, the shortcut legend hides, and the "approved baseline" qualifier hides.
- **360px:** top bar padding and gaps tighten to 8px.

Stacking order, lowest to highest: the focused option of a segmented control (1), screenshot hover hint and inline result lists (10), page header and decision bar (20), top bar (40), dropdown menus (60), skip link (100), toasts (1000).

### Named Rules

**The Screenshot Gets the Width Rule.** Every page is capped at 1120px except the one whose job is comparing images. Chrome never takes horizontal space from a screenshot.

**The Rows In One Card Rule.** A collection is one card with hairline-divided rows. Each row is the link; hover tints the whole row.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is mostly tonal: grey page, white card, slightly darker inset. Borders do the separating. Shadows exist at three strengths and are assigned by how detached a surface is, not by importance. Nothing changes its shadow on hover or press. The top bar has no shadow at all; it is 88% Card White with a backdrop blur (saturate 1.4, blur 10px) and a hairline underneath. The decision bar has no shadow either: like the page header it is a strip of the page's own Desk Grey, separated from the cards scrolling behind it by a hairline along its top edge. The active card on the review screen is marked by an outline, not by a lift. The modal scrim is a dark veil with a 2px blur. In dark the shadows are heavier black, since soft grey shadows vanish on a near-black page.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Resting** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.05)`; dark `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`): cards, default and primary buttons, inputs, selects, the search field, and a segmented control (one shadow under the whole group, not one per option). A contact shadow that only firms up the edge.
- **Attached** (`box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.04)`; dark `0 6px 16px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`): small popovers anchored to a control. Tooltips and the project search results list.
- **Floating** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.18)`; dark `0 24px 56px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`): surfaces that leave the page. Dropdown menus, modals (the submit confirm included), toasts, and the sign-in card.
- **Focus halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px` in Focus Halo): replaces the resting shadow on a focused field.
- **Decision ring** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px` in the button's tone): the Approve or Reject button on hover and once chosen.

### Named Rules

**The Shadow Means Detached Rule.** Shadow strength says how far a surface has left the page: resting for things on it, attached for things hanging off a control, floating for things above everything. Hover changes fill, never shadow.

## Shapes

Softly rounded rectangles with 1px borders. Three radii are tokens and they nest: containers at 12px (cards, modals, the screenshot card), controls and medium boxes at 8px (buttons, inputs, selects, segmented controls, notices, menus, toasts, code blocks), and inner or small pieces at 5px (small buttons, nav links, screenshots and their panes, the hover hint). A child always has a smaller radius than the surface that holds it. Tags and key caps are a tighter 4px so they read as labels, not buttons. A segmented control is one 8px box cut into options: only its two outer ends are rounded, the inner edges are square, and neighbouring options share a single 1px line. The only full pill is the admin toggle track (36px by 20px) with its round 16px knob. The brand mark is a rounded square (24px with 7px radius in the top bar, 44px with 12px radius on the sign-in card).

Borders are always 1px: Hairline for containers and dividers, Control Outline for anything you can type in or press. Ghost buttons and menu triggers keep a transparent 1px border so their size matches bordered siblings. Cards that hold rows or screenshots clip their contents to the card's corners. The keyboard focus outline is 2px in Focus Ring Indigo, offset 2px outward. The active review card wears the same outline.

### Named Rules

**The Nested Radius Rule.** 12px holds 8px holds 5px. Never give an inner element the same or a larger radius than its container.

**The Inset Ring Rule.** A focusable element that sits flush inside a clipping card (an app row, a build row, a project search result) draws its focus ring inside its own edge (2px, offset -2px), so the card cannot cut the ring off. Everything else keeps the outward ring.

## Components

Controls are small, bordered, and even. They share a 32px height and a 13px medium label, change colour over 0.12s on hover, and dim to 55% opacity with a not-allowed cursor when disabled.

### Buttons

- **Shape:** gently rounded (8px), 32px tall, 12px side padding, 7px gap between icon and label, 12px icons, resting shadow.
- **Default:** Card White fill, Control Outline border, Ink label. Hover fills with Inset Grey. Used for Cancel, Edit, Copy, the back button, and the way out offered inside an error notice ("Back to Apps", "Back to builds").
- **Primary:** Action Indigo fill and border, On Indigo label. Hover moves both to Action Indigo Hover. Used for the one committing action: Add App, Create, Save, Done, sign in, the build submit in the decision bar, and "Back to Apps" on the not-found screen.
- **Ghost:** transparent fill and border, no shadow, Secondary Ink label. Hover fills with Inset Grey and darkens the label to Ink. Used for Logout and for small row tools (app settings gear, clear, the collapse toggle, the Expand button on a collapsed card).
- **Danger:** a default button whose label is Destructive Red. It stays outlined, never filled, and always leads to a confirm dialog.
- **Sizes:** small (26px tall, 9px padding, 12px label, 5px radius), large (40px tall, 18px padding, 14px label; defined, not currently used), icon (32px square; 26px square when small).
- **Focus:** the global 2px Focus Ring Indigo outline at 2px offset.
- **Motion:** background, border, and text colour over 0.12s.

### Segmented control

A row of joined toggle buttons for choosing one of a few views. Used twice, both in the review header: the filter ("Needs review" and "All screens", each with its count in brackets) and the zoom ("Fit", "100%", "200%").

- **Shape:** one 32px tall, 8px box with the resting shadow under the whole group. Each option has 10px side padding and a Control Outline border; neighbours overlap by 1px so there is a single line between them.
- **Rest:** Card White fill, Secondary Ink label in the Control type (13px medium).
- **Hover:** Inset Grey fill, Ink label, over 0.12s.
- **Pressed:** Screenshot Mat fill, Ink label. The same quiet grey as the active nav link; the chosen option is not indigo.
- **Focus:** the global outward ring. The focused option is lifted above its neighbours so the ring is not covered by the next option's border.
- **Semantics:** every option is a real button that reports its pressed state, and the group has a name ("Screens to show", "Zoom").

### Inputs / Fields

- **Style:** 32px tall, 8px radius, Control Outline border, Card White fill, 13px text, 10px side padding, resting shadow. Placeholder in Placeholder Grey.
- **Select:** the same box with native appearance removed and a small two-triangle chevron in Meta Grey drawn 10px from the right edge.
- **Textarea:** the same box with automatic height and 8px 10px padding; the review comment starts at one row (36px minimum), the private key field at 96px in monospace.
- **Focus:** border turns Focus Ring Indigo and the shadow becomes the 3px Focus Halo. No outline.
- **Search field:** an icon and a borderless input inside one bordered box (max 360px); the box takes the focus treatment.
- **Field label:** 13px medium, stacked 6px above the control. Hints sit below in 12px Meta Grey; errors in 12px Rejected Red.
- **Disabled:** 55% opacity, not-allowed cursor.

### Checkbox and toggle

- **Checkbox:** the native control at 15px, tinted with Action Indigo. Its label sits 7px to the right in 13px Secondary Ink. Used once, for the "Diff" switch in the review header.
- **Toggle:** a 36px by 20px pill track (Control Outline off, Action Indigo on) with a 16px Card White knob that slides 16px over 0.15s. Used once, for the Admin switch on the users list. When it is disabled (your own row) only the track dims to 55%; the label stays at full strength because it still says whose row this is.

### Cards / Containers

- **Corner Style:** softly rounded (12px).
- **Background:** Card White on Desk Grey.
- **Shadow Strategy:** resting shadow only; see Elevation.
- **Border:** 1px Hairline.
- **Internal Padding:** rows and card sections use 14px 18px (16px 18px for admin rows and the screenshot card). A header and body pair (14px 18px header with a hairline below, 16px 18px body) is defined but not currently used.

### Tags

- **Style:** 19px tall, 4px radius, 6px side padding, 11px medium text, 1px border, 4px gap to an optional 9.5px icon. Default tone is Draft Grey.
- **Tones:** warning, success, info, release, danger. Each swaps fill, border, and text together (The Tag Triplet Rule). See the status mapping table.
- **Sizes:** build status tags are 24px tall, both on build rows and in the review header. The screen number tag is monospace.
- **Wording:** build statuses say what happened to the changes: "Changes Approved" and "Changes Rejected", matching the screen status "Changes Approved". The shorter "Approved" and "Rejected" are kept for the per-screen review badge.

### Notices

- **Style:** a full-width banner: 8px radius, 10px 14px padding, 13px Secondary Ink text, leading 13px icon, optional actions on the right. Default is the neutral triplet.
- **Tones:** danger, warning, info swap the fill and border and colour the icon; body text stays Secondary Ink and bold text is Ink.
- **In use:** only the danger notice. A failed sign-in; an app or its builds that could not be loaded on the builds page; a build that could not be loaded on the review screen. The load failures carry a warning triangle, say what may have happened, and, where the page is a dead end, offer one default button as the way out, centred vertically against the text. A failed builds list shows its notice inside the list card with a 16px margin.

### Navigation

- **Top bar:** sticky, 46px, translucent Card White with blur, hairline below. Left: the brand (24px inverted mark, then "PixelCI" at 14px weight 650). Then the primary nav. Right: theme menu, administration menu (admins only), Logout as a ghost button.
- **Nav link:** 13px medium Secondary Ink, 6px 10px padding, 5px radius. Hover fills Inset Grey; the active route fills Screenshot Mat. Both darken the label to Ink. No underline, no indicator bar.
- **Skip link:** an Action Indigo block (8px radius) that drops in from above the bar on keyboard focus.
- **Page header:** sticky under the top bar. A back button (a 32px square default button with a left arrow) precedes the title on nested pages.
- **Review header:** the page header of the review screen. After the back button and the app name comes a context line in 12px Secondary Ink with small Meta Grey icons: the branch, then the commit (short hash in monospace Ink, then the subject), then the 24px build status tag. The commit is a link when the app knows its commit URL and turns Link Indigo on hover; the full subject is in a tooltip. The tools sit on the right. See Layout for how it wraps.
- **Mobile:** the wordmark hides below 640px; there is no drawer because there is one nav link.

### Dropdown menu and theme menu

- **Trigger:** a 32px ghost-like button (transparent border, Secondary Ink). Hover and the open state fill Inset Grey. In the top bar it is a 32px icon square.
- **Menu:** Card White, Hairline border, 8px radius, 5px padding, floating shadow, at least 200px wide, 6px below the trigger, right-aligned by default.
- **Items:** 13px, 7px 9px padding, 6px radius, a 14px Meta Grey leading icon. Hover and keyboard focus fill Inset Grey without an outline. Group labels use Label caps.
- **Motion:** fades and slides 3px down into place over 0.1s.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move, Home and End jump, Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger.
- **Theme menu:** three radio items (System, Light, Dark). The current one shows an Action Indigo check on the right, and the trigger icon reflects the mode (half circle, sun, moon). All of its icons are the solid style, so they carry the same weight as the rest of the bar.

### Modal

- **Scrim:** rgba(10, 12, 16, 0.42) with a 2px blur.
- **Panel:** Card White, Hairline border, 12px radius, floating shadow, contents clipped.
- **Form dialog:** a 520px column, 24px padding, 16px gaps, a 17px semibold title, stacked fields, and an action row aligned right (Cancel, then the primary). A destructive action sits alone on the left of that row. It scrolls inside itself when taller than the viewport.
- **Behaviour:** the app's own dialogs (the create and edit forms and the submit confirm) behave as modal dialogs. The panel is announced as a dialog named by its title. Focus moves in when it opens, to the first editable field or else the first control. Tab cycles inside it. Escape closes it exactly as Cancel does. On close, focus goes back to the control that opened it, or to the page if that control is gone.
- **Alert and confirm:** a semibold header with a hairline below, 16px 18px body in Secondary Ink, and an Inset Grey footer strip with right-aligned buttons.
- **Loading:** a small modal holding only the indigo spinner, used while a page loads.

### Submit confirm

The dialog between the decision bar's button and the build being submitted. A 440px column with 24px padding and 16px gaps (18px padding at 640px and below): a 17px semibold title that asks the question ("Approve this build?", or "Submit with 2 rejections?"), one Secondary Ink paragraph that says what will happen (unreviewed screens are approved as well; rejected screens fail the build; the CI job is re-run), and a right-aligned action row with Cancel and then the primary. The primary repeats the label of the button that opened the dialog ("Approve build" or "Submit review") instead of a generic "Confirm". Focus starts on Cancel, so submitting takes a deliberate choice, and Escape cancels.

### Toast

- **Style:** Card White, Hairline border, 8px radius, floating shadow, 13px text, 11px 14px padding, at least 260px wide.
- **Progress:** a 2px bar along the bottom on an Inset Grey track: Action Indigo by default, Approved Green for success, Rejected Red for errors.
- **In use:** "Copied to clipboard", shown for 2 seconds, and the confirmation that a review with rejections was submitted, shown for 5 seconds on the builds page.

### Tooltip

- **Style:** a dark chip in both themes (#1d1f25 fill, #f2f3f5 text; in dark #2a2d35 with a 1px #3a3e48 border). 12px text, 6px 9px padding, 6px radius, max 320px, attached shadow. Known gap, not a rule to copy: these four colours are hard-coded rather than tokens.
- **In use:** the full commit subject on truncated commit lines, Expand or Collapse on the screenshot card toggle, and the shortcut reminder on the Diff switch.

### Loader and skeleton

- **Loader:** a spinning circle-notch icon in Action Indigo, centred, at five sizes (20px, 24px, 36px default, 48px, 60px). Under reduced motion it keeps turning but slows to one turn per 3 seconds.
- **Skeleton:** a 10px tall bar with 4px radius that shimmers between Inset Grey and Screenshot Mat over 1.4s, and stops under reduced motion. Defined, not currently used; pages show the loader modal instead.

### Empty state

A centred column inside a card: a 26px Meta Grey icon, then a 15px semibold line in Secondary Ink ("No apps", "No builds found", "Nothing in this build needs review."). At least 240px tall, 14px gap. No illustration. When there is something the reader can do next, a plain 14px Meta Grey sentence under the title says so ("Add an app to start comparing screenshots.", or, for someone who cannot add one, that an administrator needs to). The not-found screen is this same pattern in a card of its own: a compass icon, "Page not found", one sentence, and the one place an empty state carries a button (primary, "Back to Apps"), because the page has no header to hold it.

### List rows

- **App row:** name (14px semibold) on the left; build count (12px Meta Grey) and, for admins, a small ghost gear on the right. At least 60px tall, 14px 18px padding.
- **Build row:** a five-column grid: branch, commit (monospace short hash over a 12px truncated subject), author, date (12px Meta Grey), and a 24px status tag with an icon, right-aligned in a 140px column, wide enough for "Changes Approved". Each cell starts with a small Meta Grey icon. At least 72px tall, 14px 18px padding. The commit cell is its own link and turns Link Indigo on hover.
- **Admin row:** name (semibold), a tag or 12px meta beside it, and actions on the right (Edit and Delete buttons, or the Admin toggle). 16px 18px padding; wraps when narrow.
- **Project search result:** a full-width button inside the attached results list under the Project field: the project name over its path, left-aligned, 8px 10px padding, no border or fill of its own.
- **Shared behaviour:** hairline between rows, Row Hover fill over 0.12s on clickable rows, pointer cursor, focusable with Enter to open. App rows, build rows, and project results draw their focus ring inside their own edge (The Inset Ring Rule).

### Screenshot comparison card

The signature component: one card per screen on the review screen.

- **Card:** Card White, Hairline border, 12px radius, resting shadow, 16px 18px padding, 14px between sections, each section after the first opened by a hairline.
- **Meta row:** a monospace number tag, the screen name (14px semibold), and at the far right one tag: the screen's status, or the Approved or Rejected badge once it has been reviewed. A card that can fold gains a 24px ghost chevron toggle at the start of the row.
- **Comparison:** two equal columns with a 6px gap, labelled "Reference build" (with the qualifier "approved baseline") and "New build" in Label caps, centred. Each column is its own scroll pane and the two are locked together; see Layout for the lock, the height cap, and the zoom modes.
- **Pane:** a Screenshot Mat panel with 5px corners holding the screenshot, centred, also with 5px corners. Scrollbars are thin, in Control Outline, with their gutter reserved so the two panes stay the same width.
- **Diff:** the diff image is laid exactly over the new build while the new build turns transparent in place, so nothing shifts. The Diff checkbox in the header turns it on for every card, Shift+click on either screenshot does the same, and holding D flips it for as long as the key is down. Hovering either screenshot fades in an inverted hint chip (Ink fill, Desk Grey text, 11px, a small layers icon, 90% opacity, 0.15s) at the top right of that pane, naming both shortcuts. A new screen has nothing to diff against and says so beside its column label.
- **Placeholders:** a missing or failed side shows an Inset Grey panel (at least 180px tall) with a centred Meta Grey sentence no wider than 340px. Each one explains itself: a new screen has no reference yet and approving makes it the baseline; a removed screen is no longer in the build; an image could not be loaded.
- **Review bar:** below a hairline, a comment field that grows, then Approve (success triplet) and Reject (danger triplet), each at least 36px tall. On hover, and once chosen, the border takes the full tone and gains the 1px decision ring. It appears only while the build is awaiting review; every other build is read-only.
- **Collapsed, reviewed:** after a decision the card folds to its meta row plus a summary line: the comment (or an italic "No comment" in Meta Grey) and a small ghost "Expand" button. The next undecided card then becomes the active card and scrolls to the top, smoothly unless reduced motion is requested.
- **Collapsed, unchanged:** a screen with no changes is a single line: chevron, number, name, and its "No Changes" tag. It is listed only under "All screens". Expanding it shows the new screenshot alone, without column labels.
- **Removed screens:** listed under "Needs review" with a "Removed" tag, the reference on the left and a placeholder on the right. There is nothing to approve, so there is no review bar.

### Active card

One card on the review screen is the active one: the card the keyboard shortcuts act on. It is marked with the focus ring (2px Focus Ring Indigo, 2px offset) drawn around the whole card; nothing else about the card changes. A card becomes active when it is clicked or touched, when focus moves into it, or when J or K steps to it. J and K move down and up the list and bring the card to the top if it is not fully in view. A and R approve or reject the active card, but only if it is on screen; if it has scrolled away, the first press just moves the ring to a card that is visible. Shortcuts are ignored while typing in a field and while a dialog or menu is open. Each decision is also announced to screen readers along with the running count.

### Decision bar

The sticky strip at the bottom of the review screen that holds the build-level decision. Desk Grey with a hairline along the top and no shadow.

- **Progress:** 13px medium Ink text on the left: how many screens are reviewed out of how many, then how many unreviewed ones will be approved on submit, then how many were removed.
- **Legend:** the keyboard legend fills the middle.
- **Action:** the screen's single primary button, pushed to the right. Its label is the outcome: "Approve build", or "Submit review" as soon as any screen is rejected. The counts stay in the progress text and the confirm, not on the button. It is disabled while a review is being saved, and it opens the submit confirm.
- **Narrow:** wraps at 900px and below.

### Keyboard legend

A line of 12px Meta Grey text in the decision bar listing the review shortcuts: J and K for next and previous, A to approve, R to reject, D for the diff, separated by middle dots. Each key is a key cap: Inset Grey fill, Hairline border, 4px radius, 2px 4px padding, at least 18px wide, 11px monospace in Secondary Ink. Each key and its action stay together when the line wraps. Hidden at 640px and below.

### Auth page

Used for sign-in and first-run setup. A single card centred in the viewport (max 400px, 480px for setup; 32px 30px 28px padding; 14px gaps; floating shadow) holding the 44px brand mark, a centred Display title, a 13px Secondary Ink subtitle, an optional danger notice, and full-width primary buttons with the provider's brand icon. The theme menu sits alone in the top right corner. Behind the card, and only here: a 32px grid of hairlines with a faint indigo glow at the top, masked to fade out toward the edges, at 70% opacity.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** take every colour from the custom properties, so a new surface works in light and dark without a theme branch.
- **Do** give each page header, dialog, or decision bar one filled indigo button for its committing action, and keep the rest default or ghost (The One Indigo Rule).
- **Do** show status as a tag with all three tone tokens and a word in it, using the status mapping table (The Tag Triplet Rule).
- **Do** keep controls at 32px tall with 13px medium labels so buttons, inputs, selects, and segmented controls align on one row.
- **Do** put collections in one card with hairline-divided rows, and make the whole row the link (The Rows In One Card Rule).
- **Do** pick a shadow by how detached the surface is: resting, attached, or floating (The Shadow Means Detached Rule).
- **Do** nest radii 12px, 8px, 5px from container to control to inner piece (The Nested Radius Rule).
- **Do** set hashes, IDs, snippets, and URLs in the monospace stack and everything else in Inter (The Machine Text Rule).
- **Do** keep the visible keyboard focus: a 2px Focus Ring Indigo outline at 2px offset, the 3px Focus Halo on fields, and the ring drawn inside the edge for rows in a clipping card (The Inset Ring Rule).
- **Do** keep motion to short colour and opacity changes (0.1s to 0.15s) and honour reduced motion the way the skeleton, the spinner, and the scroll to the next card do.
- **Do** give screenshots the width: a full-width container on the review screen, and natural size as the upper limit of Fit.
- **Do** keep the two panes of a comparison locked together, and keep a pane short enough that the card's name and review bar stay on screen with it.
- **Do** name the outcome on a committing button and repeat that name on the confirm ("Approve build", "Submit review"), and start focus on Cancel when the action cannot be taken back.
- **Do** open the app's own dialogs through the shared modal wrapper so they get a dialog role, a focus trap, Escape, and focus restore.
- **Do** say what went wrong in a danger notice in the page, with a way out when the page is a dead end, and give an empty state a next-step line when there is a next step.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use indigo for status, or a status tone for a general-purpose button. Approve and Reject are the only toned buttons (The Borrowed Fill Rule).
- **Don't** fill a tag or notice with a solid tone; the fill is always the soft token with the tone on text and border.
- **Don't** change a shadow on hover, or add one to the top bar, the page header, or the decision bar; hover changes fill only and sticky chrome is separated by a hairline.
- **Don't** fill a destructive button red. Delete is an outlined button with a Destructive Red label, followed by a confirm dialog.
- **Don't** let Fit scale a screenshot above its natural width, smooth the 200% view (it is pixelated on purpose), or lay chrome over a screenshot; the hover hint chip is the only overlay.
- **Don't** fill the pressed option of a segmented control with indigo; it takes Screenshot Mat, like the active nav link.
- **Don't** put counts on the submit button; they belong in the progress text and the confirm.
- **Don't** use uppercase text outside the two places it exists: comparison column labels and dropdown group labels.
- **Don't** reuse the grid-and-glow background outside the auth page.
- **Don't** hard-code a colour, radius, or shadow where a token exists.
- **Don't** copy the known gaps below into new work.

### Known gaps, not rules to copy

- **Tailwind leftovers.** Tailwind v4 is still imported globally (including its reset). Eleven of its layout utilities remain in templates, down from fourteen (`flex`, `flex-col`, `items-center`, `justify-end`, `gap-2`, `gap-4`, `w-full`, `pr-6`, `truncate`, `font-mono`, `text-inherit`), almost all in the build list. The review screen is down to one, `truncate`, on two spans of its header. The loader sets its sizes through Tailwind in rem while everything else is px. No Tailwind colour, radius, or shadow utilities are used.
- **Weight 650 is not loaded.** Page titles and the wordmark ask for 650, and the stack names 'Inter var' first, but only static Inter 400, 500, 600, and 700 are shipped, so 650 renders as 700.
- **Hard-coded colours.** The tooltip's four colours and the modal scrim are literals. The page background is repeated as literals in `index.html` (deliberate, to avoid a flash before styles load). The favicon uses blues, a red, and a slate that are not in the palette.
- **Off-scale radii.** 4px (tags, key caps, skeleton), 6px (tooltip, menu items), 7px (brand mark), 3px (focus outline), and a literal 12px on the auth mark sit beside the three radius tokens.
- **Off-ramp sizes.** 15px (empty state title), 17px (dialog title), 18px (full-page error), 12.5px (unused card header subtitle), and two near-identical title sizes (19px and 20px). Status tags are 19px or 24px depending on the screen.
- **Mono size drift.** Commit hashes in build rows still use Tailwind's `font-mono` utility, which applies the mono stack but not the 0.92em reduction given to inline code, so they render at the surrounding 14px. The review header now uses the shared mono style, so the same hash is 0.92em of 12px there.
- **Shared and borrowed tones.** "Processing" and "Needs Review" builds share the warning tone and differ only by icon. "New" screens use a token named `release`.
- **Unused primitives.** Defined in the stylesheet but not used by any screen: card header and body, large button, link button, skeleton, warning and info notices, and the menu separator and meta block.
- **Local one-offs.** Nine pieces are styled inside the screens that use them rather than as shared primitives: the Approve and Reject buttons, the admin toggle, the search field, the back button, and now the segmented control, the submit confirm, the decision bar, the key cap, and the active ring. The 1280px cap on a lone screenshot is a literal.
- **Dialogs outside the wrapper.** The alert and confirm dialogs that come from the foundation library (delete confirmations, error alerts, the notices after a submit) and the loading modal do not go through the shared modal wrapper, so they do not get its dialog role, focus trap, or focus restore.
